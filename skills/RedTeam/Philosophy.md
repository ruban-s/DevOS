# RedTeam Guiding Bets

## Where It Comes From

Drill borrowed from military practice: keep one crew whose whole job is to defeat the plan before anyone else gets the chance.

## The Bet That Matters

**Breaking is not the point — locating the one break that matters is.** A plan rarely dies from ten small nicks. It dies when a single quiet premise gives way and everything stacked on it follows.

Watch for that shape in four disguises:
- A background belief treated as fact, which is wrong
- A step that claims to follow but doesn't
- Two different problems handled as if they were one
- A past case everyone forgot that already settled the question

## What Counts as Good

- The friendly restatement is so fair the author nods along
- The rebuttal hits that fair version, not a weakened copy
- Unrelated reviewers keep landing on the same cracks independently
- The reader walks away with at least one angle they hadn't considered
