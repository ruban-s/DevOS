# Evolution Cycle

## Done Looks Like

An evolved portfolio of hard-to-vary explanations — a brainstorm with lineage. Every survivor traces its ancestry, advances a distinct causal hypothesis, states its relation to the target phenomenon explicitly, and reports its test status.

## The Full Cycle

1. **CONSUME** — gather observations, anomalies, mechanisms, constraints, and the serious rivals.
2. **DREAM** — propose strange but structured causal hypotheses; keep fantasy, hypothesis, and evidence in separate piles.
3. **DAYDREAM** — run counterfactuals, role reversals, scale changes, and causal leaps over the material.
4. **SALON** — collide independent disciplinary viewpoints; forbid premature consensus.
5. **STEAL** — carry mechanisms across domains with deliberate imperfection; name the productive mismatch.
6. **MATE** — recombine causal families and mutate assumptions, dependencies, scale, or time horizon; log the lineage.
7. **TEST** — throw candidates against observations, constraints, evidence, and feasible interventions.
8. **EXPLANATORY SELECTION** — apply ExplanatorySelection.md and HardToVary.md. Demote whatever merely fits, relabels, or decorates the phenomenon. Advance only constrained causal cores with necessary parts, distinctive counterfactuals, and risky discriminators.
9. **SELECT** — keep a diverse portfolio of hard-to-vary explanatory survivors plus one controlled high-upside wildcard; salvage useful mechanisms from the rejected lineages.
10. **EVOLVE** — breed the next population from survivors and salvaged mechanisms.
11. **META-LEARN** — revise disciplines, mutations, tests, and selection rules in light of what survived and what merely looked attractive.

## Output Contract

Return the phenomenon and its evidence boundary; the survivor portfolio (causal core, lineage, necessary components, hard-to-vary tests, next experiment per survivor); wildcards and retired mechanisms; the evolution report; and the revised selection rule.

## Stop Conditions

Halt when the cycle budget runs out, when evidence decisively favors a portfolio, when constraints eliminate the field, or when marginal explanatory learning collapses. Name the stopping condition — and what would justify resuming.
