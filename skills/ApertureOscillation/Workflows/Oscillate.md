# Oscillate — one question, two zooms, one gap read

## Charter

Resolve a single question twice — once led by the part, once led by the whole — then publish where the answers part ways. The parting points are the product: named tensions, a sizing call, and a coherence verdict neither zoom reaches alone.

## Entry points

1. **Explicit ask:** "use aperture oscillation on this", "oscillate scope on this".
2. **Planning selection:** a planning run calls for scope-coherence analysis before requirements or approach lock.
3. **Peer delegation:** another skill needs a part-versus-whole check first.

## What to bring

- **Build target:** the concrete item under design or decision.
- **Surrounding intent:** the encompassing aim or system the target serves.
- **Standing context (optional):** existing criteria, locked decisions, hard constraints.

Absent explicit inputs, recover them from the thread: the immediate ask becomes the target; the enclosing project or product direction becomes the intent. When target and intent prove to be the same proposition at the same scale, stop and point at IterativeDepth — no scope delta means no oscillation.

## The finished shape

A complete oscillation demonstrates three traits:

1. **Close-up resolution** — the question answered with the component primary and the system ambient: native shape, idioms, interfaces, what a fine standalone build looks like.
2. **Pulled-back resolution** — the *same* question answered with the system primary and the component derived: coherence demands, neighbor contracts, obligations only the wide view imposes.
3. **Gap resolution** — each divergence named with a handling proposal, closing on ALIGNED or DIVERGENT. Concord is reportable: state plainly that the zooms agree.

The two resolutions must differ in what they center. A pulled-back pass that paraphrases the close-up means the zoom never moved — push each framing until it yields something the other could not.

## Report mold

Open by framing both inputs, then follow this contour:

```
🔭 APERTURE OSCILLATION
 🎯 Tactical Target: [the concrete thing — 1-2 sentences]
 🌐 Strategic Context: [the encompassing aim — 1-2 sentences]

── Narrow (component-first) ──
[what the part wants to be on its own terms]

── Wide (system-first) ──
[what the whole requires of the part]

── Synthesis ──
Divergences: [each close-up vs pulled-back split]

⚡ DESIGN TENSIONS:
[each tension plus handling proposal]

📋 ISC IMPLICATIONS:
[criteria the sweep adds, sharpens, or rules out]

Coherence: [ALIGNED | DIVERGENT — one-line verdict]
💡 Key Insight: [the non-obvious yield of the two-zoom read]
```

## Where it slots into planning

- **Ahead of requirements:** target = the incoming request, intent = project and conversation context. Tensions land before criteria are drafted, so criteria absorb them.
- **Ahead of approach lock:** target = the candidate approach, intent = wider product or system aims. Confirms the approach honors both part and whole before commitment.

## Pairing with IterativeDepth

Sequence, don't choose: IterativeDepth first to chart the problem from several analytic sides, ApertureOscillation second to seat the answer in its system. Deep or comprehensive efforts benefit most from the pair — breadth of problem view, then fit of solution.

## Briefing a subagent

When handing the sweep to a spawned agent, pass both inputs plus the finished-shape definition:

```
CONTEXT: Perform Aperture Oscillation — resolve one question at two
zooms to expose part-logic versus whole-logic tensions.

TACTICAL TARGET: {concrete thing under design}
STRATEGIC CONTEXT: {encompassing aim or system direction}

DELIVERABLE: a part-first resolution, a system-first resolution
of the same question, and a synthesis enumerating each split
with a handling proposal and a coherence verdict (ALIGNED |
DIVERGENT). Include design tensions and criteria implications.
SLA: Complete within 45 seconds.
```
