# Horizon Read Skeleton

Follow this shape when drafting or refreshing any horizon read.

## Header Block

```yaml
---
horizon: {6-month | 1-year | 2-year | 3-year | 5-year | 7-year | 10-year | 15-year | 20-year | 30-year | 50-year}
last_updated: YYYY-MM-DD
version: {integer, bump on every refresh}
confidence: {high | medium | low}
confidence_note: {one line on why this grade}
---
```

## Mandatory Chapters (every one ships)

### 1. Opening Brief (~300 words)
The window at a glance. Which 5–7 traits define it? What moved most since today? Must read standalone as a briefing.

### 2. Power and Politics (~600 words)
- Rivalry among great powers (US, China, EU, Russia, India, rising blocs)
- Alliance drift and reformation
- Flashpoints and conflict arcs
- Governing models and institutional change
- Trade blocs and economic alignment

### 3. Technology and Machine Intelligence (~800 words)
- Capability arc (reasoning, autonomy, multimodal, embodied)
- Compute and infrastructure direction
- Breakthroughs plausibly arriving or already stirring
- Adoption tempo and social absorption
- Platform and digital-infrastructure shape
- Security-landscape drift

### 4. Markets and Livelihoods (~600 words)
- Global output and growth arc
- Which economic models gain or lose
- Workforce reshaping (automation, new trades)
- Money rails (crypto, CBDC, legacy)
- Sectors breaking or being born
- Distribution and inequality motion

### 5. People and Culture (~500 words)
- Population motion and migration
- Value and norm drift
- Schooling reshaped
- Information diet and media shape
- Wellbeing and mental-health direction
- Urban versus rural pull

### 6. Climate and Resources (~500 words)
- Warming arc and milestone watch
- Energy-transition pace
- Scarcity and access fights
- Adaptation versus mitigation balance
- Ecosystem and biodiversity drift

### 7. Safety and Strife (~500 words)
- Conventional-conflict picture
- Cyber and information operations
- Bio-risk and pandemic readiness
- Nuclear and WMD posture
- Non-state actor shape
- Critical-infrastructure exposure

### 8. Wildcards (~400 words)
- 3–5 sub-10% likelihood, outsized-impact turns
- Per turn: tripwires, likelihood guess, damage scale, recovery arc
- At least one bright wildcard (breakthrough, surprise cooperation)
- At least one bleak wildcard (systemic snap, escalation)

### 9. Trust Calibration (~300 words)
- Overall trust in this read
- Five uncertainties likeliest to break major calls
- Which chapters run hottest versus coldest, and why
- Which fresh evidence would most rewrite this read

## Voice Rules

- **Hedge honestly:** "likely," "projected," "current arc suggests," "if trends hold"
- **Show the why:** never assert a trend without its engine (headcounts, capability curves, past rhymes)
- **Name names:** countries, firms, systems, figures wherever possible
- **Grade certainty:** demographics run near-certain; realignment guesses run speculative — label the difference
- **Near reads** (6mo–3yr): lean on live data and dated events
- **Middle reads** (5yr–10yr): mix trajectory math with structural read
- **Far reads** (15yr–50yr): lean on deep forces, megatrends, scenario play

## Floor

Each read should land near 4,000–6,000 words (~8–12 pages). No chapter under its guidance above. Substance over sweep — deep beats wide.
