# TestIdea Answer Shape

Shape every finished TestIdea pass like this.

## Masthead

```markdown
# 🌍 World Threat Model Analysis

**Idea/Strategy:** {caller's input, compressed to 1-2 sentences}
**Gear:** {Fast | Standard | Deep}
**Date:** {YYYY-MM-DD}
**Reads Last Refreshed:** {oldest read's date}
```

## Lead Call

```markdown
## Executive Verdict

**Overall Assessment:** {1-2 sentences on standing across all windows}

**Strongest Window:** {which horizon(s) favor it and why, in 1 sentence}
**Roughest Window:** {which horizon(s) threaten it and why, in 1 sentence}
**Recommendation:** {actionable 1-2 sentence steer}
```

## Window-by-Window

One fenced block PER horizon of the 11:

```markdown
## ━━━ {HORIZON} Horizon ━━━

**Verdict:** {🟢 FAVORABLE | 🟡 NEUTRAL | 🔴 UNFAVORABLE}
**Confidence:** {HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW}

### Key Factors
- {Read factor that bears on the idea}
- {Factor 2}
- {Factor 3}
- {Factor 4 when earned}

### Analysis
{2-4 paragraphs of close work. How does this window's world
touch the idea — what thrives, what snaps, what bends?}

### Assumptions Tested (FirstPrinciples)
- **Hard constraint:** {limit that cannot move}
- **Soft constraint:** {limit that could drift}
- **Assumption challenged:** {bet the idea needs that may lapse}

### Adversarial Findings (RedTeam)
- **Attack vector:** {toughest case against the idea here}
- **Steelman:** {toughest defense of the idea here}
```

## Through-Line (Standard and Deep gears only)

```markdown
## Cross-Horizon Synthesis

### Timeline of Viability
{A story arc for viability over time.
Beyond "good then bad" — name the engines, tipping points,
and what flips each transition.}

### Critical Inflection Points
1. **{Year/Period}:** {what shifts and why the idea feels it}
2. **{Year/Period}:** {second shift}
3. **{Year/Period}:** {third when earned}

### Adaptation Recommendations
{How the idea must mutate to survive the far windows, and when}
```

## Deliberation Record (Deep gear only)

```markdown
## Council Deliberation

### Strongest Argument FOR
{The bull case across all windows}

### Strongest Argument AGAINST
{The bear case across all windows}

### Points of Consensus
{Where every voice landed together}

### Unresolved Tensions
{Where honest disagreement stands}
```

## Sign-Off

```markdown
---
*Analysis generated by World Threat Model Harness | Reads version: {dates} | Gear: {gear}*
*For refreshes: "update world models" | For other depth: "test [idea] deep/fast"*
```
